## Landscape

An ancient canopy of pale bark, ferns and fungi surrounds rare supernatural inhabitants. Petrol green and violet distinguish a strongly magical forest direction.

## Life and use

Working development focuses on the edge: guides, collectors and households who know useful routes without mastering the whole forest. Livelihoods need habitats and handling practices before rare materials become trade goods. The canopy can retain strangeness without every inhabitant serving one hidden power.

## Connections

Compared with the [Underbough](underbough.md), Vesperra carries more overt supernatural possibility. Compared with [Ru’Lora](ru-lora.md), it remains a living landscape. [Magic](../magic.md) can be locally specific; no cause links all three forests.

## Open questions

The identity of rare inhabitants, exact risks and reasons for entering are open. No approved forest faction or safe extraction industry follows from the paintings.
