## Landscape

Honey-colored sandstone, lapis glazing, copper and cultivated green define terraced garden settlements. Irrigation and solar horticulture give the selected region its craft identity.

## Life and use

Associated with [Ellion’ar sun-elven traditions](../peoples.md#ellionar--sun-elves), Ellionar develops cultivation through skilled work and inherited practice. Working lore includes household plots, public channels, shade and workshops, with civic maintenance and religious observance overlapping without being identical.

## Connections

[Istrava](../settlements.md#istrava) is one proposed settlement. Its [hushlime](../magic.md#hushlime) practice and the [Three Measures agreement](../three-measures.md) connect material knowledge to daily obligations. A dryland margin with [Sereward](sereward.md) is proposed, not surveyed.

## Open questions

The author’s sun-elven homeland is temperate; this regional direction is a deliberate adaptation opportunity. Exact extent, political unity and plant species remain open.
