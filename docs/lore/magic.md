# Magic and materials

**Status:** creative direction with individually marked anchors and working practices. No universal magic system is selected.

Magic in Vaelora is encountered through particular materials, procedures, places and relationships. Its effects have consequences for bodies and livelihoods. A successful practice may outlive its explanation, and different traditions need not share one mechanism.

## Practice and knowledge

Apprenticeship, experimentation and guarded instruction distribute expertise unevenly. A knowledgeable practitioner can be genuinely capable without understanding every tradition. Interrupted rites, handling mistakes, obligations and jurisdiction can constrain power. The source stories inspire these possibilities; they do not require that all Vaeloran magic operate through contracts.

## Hushlime

**Working proposal.** An [Istrava](settlements.md#istrava) horticultural limewash prepared with copper at dusk. Once fully dried, it cools an enclosed seed chamber beyond ordinary whitewash for several nights. Wet material blisters skin and spoils seed. It neither summons rain nor multiplies food.

Gardeners dispute whether its action comes from retained dusk, copper or a living ferment. An old persistent sample appeared in a calibration exercise. Its preparation date and extraordinary behavior are not yet established wiki history; the exercise does not prove an ancient cooling network.

## Living light

**Source inspiration, not selected Vaeloran resource.** The Dreadvael manuscript’s sunstones contain living microcosms; damage can destroy their light. This suggests a useful distinction between a material that can be mined and something that must be kept alive. No trade, habitat or harvesting industry for such stones is established in Vaelora yet.

## Essence and prohibition

**Selected anchor.** [Ru’Lora](regions/ru-lora.md) contains lingering essence associated with [Colossumbra](colossumbra.md), alongside toxic dust and salt caverns. Demon refusal to enter is part of the selected direction. It establishes a specific sacred boundary, not immunity to every danger or a universal demon law.

God-flesh, blood rites and particular reagents in the source manuscript remain adaptation material unless expressly adopted. The wiki does not promise safe access to divine remains.

## The spring-name story

**Proposed in-world belief.** At [Tassel’s Ennd](settlements.md#tassels-ennd), tellers describe a woman lending her name to a wandering spring so people could find it. Her family recognized her face but could no longer call her by name. She negotiated its return with the wet season; her sister called her to supper using a bell until then.

Some tellings add a marriage; others end with rain and the name spoken in the next room. Wet-season bells and a cup left for a traveler preserve the custom in the settlement draft. No author-facing fact establishes that the bargain happened or that names govern every spring.

## Unwritten questions

The nature of divine beings, shared origins of magical traditions, reliable resurrection and the full status of lunar rites remain open. Disputed explanation should not become permission for any effect the plot happens to need.
