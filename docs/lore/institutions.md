# Factions and institutions

**Status:** working political proposals. None is a new implemented gameplay faction.

Institutions bind people through useful work, shared claims and enforceable obligations. Their members can differ in ancestry, region and interpretation of their purpose. A faction should acquire history and constituencies before its name becomes a universal ideology.

## Wider faction directions

| Working name | Proposed organizing concern | Unsettled boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common Hearth | Provision, continuity of household life, shared protection | Membership, leaders, territory and relationship to the Store Fellowship |
| Open Passage | Movement, exchange and obligations beyond one home | Limits of access, enforcement and relationship to the Joined Road |
| Continuance | Preserving practices and knowledge across loss | What it preserves, whose authority it recognizes and how it treats revision |
| Boughward | Stewardship of inhabited woodland | Which forests, constituencies and actual duties define it |

These concerns are development directions added here, not evidence that the four names already describe established world powers. None commands an entire region. Continuance and Boughward require more development before assigning them events, uniforms or historical influence.

## The Store Fellowship

A proposed association among growers, millers, garden households and storekeepers in the [Three Measures corridor](three-measures.md). Members maintain emergency stocks and buy replacement tools together. Contributions can be goods or labor, with hardship concessions. Settlement delegates choose purchasing officers; members may inspect tallies.

Its promise is that a bad season need not destroy a household. Its weakness is unequal influence: long-established households have more say than migrant contributors. Nella Imbray of [Pellit](settlements.md#pellit) argues for membership based on contribution and dependence rather than property.

During the water dispute, delegates prioritize gardens while some members warn that a closed road will consume the very reserves they protect. Deren Voskel’s undisclosed advance puts the association’s trust at risk. The Fellowship tests a Common Hearth theme but is not declared its branch or predecessor.

## The Joined Road

A proposed association of guides, carriers, lodging houses and trading partners. Standing depends on witnessed deliveries and assistance. Road speakers hear claims; refusing a carrier’s guarantees can strand their business.

Its promise is that obligations can follow a person beyond home. Powerful proprietors nevertheless decide how much risk hired crews must accept. Tessa Orven demands a reliable water quantity at [Tassel’s Ennd](settlements.md#tassels-ennd), while drivers question the loads sent into a shortage.

The Road funds coordination and argues for the caravan guarantee. Members disagree about armed enforcement. It tests an Open Passage theme without settling that faction’s structure.

## Present tension

The two associations overlap: a Pellit merchant can belong to both. Their [dispute](three-measures.md) comes from incompatible obligations and deliberate escalation by particular officers. It does not make every member on either side an enemy.
