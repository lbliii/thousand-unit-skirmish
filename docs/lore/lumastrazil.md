# Lumastrazil

**Status:** selected lunar/divine anchor; fate, genealogy and surviving followers remain open.

Lumastrazil is associated with [Soman’ar lunar traditions](peoples.md#somanar--moon-elves) and the [Sombral Mere](regions/sombral-mere.md). Her silence shapes the selected direction: practices persist while their relationship to an absent or unresponsive divinity is uncertain.

Silence does not by itself establish death, imprisonment, hostility or the failure of every lunar rite. Those are distinct claims that need separate decisions. The moon-elven population’s condition is likewise not inferred from the number of buildings in concept art.

## Competing source versions

The primary Colossumbra manuscript calls her his older sister through the god’s speech. Other project notes give a different degree of kinship and accounts involving a dark star. The wiki records the difference without promoting all versions into simultaneous fact. [Colossumbra](colossumbra.md) is an established linked anchor; the exact relationship remains open.

## Traditions under development

Opals, nocturnal gardens and reflected light connect lunar practice to specific materials and landscapes. Working development may include custodians preserving observances without claiming direct divine instruction. Whether those custodians are surviving Soman’ar, descendants or people who inherited another culture’s practices remains undecided.
