# Colossumbra

**Status:** selected supernatural anchor; wider biography and cosmological relationships remain open.

Colossumbra is the immense shadow god whose remains lie within [Ru’Lora](regions/ru-lora.md). The petrified jungle, salt caverns, lingering essence and sacred refusal of demons give those remains a physical and religious presence. He is not simply an emblem on an ancient ruin.

## What the world retains

The remains make Ru’Lora exceptional among Vaelora’s regions. Interior hazards limit access; a proposed living fringe does not make the whole jungle safely inhabited. Accounts can circulate through [scholarship and travel](world.md) without providing a reliable description of every chamber.

## Source accounts

In the author’s primary manuscript, Vel hears Colossumbra and attempts a bodily, materially difficult act involving divine flesh. The god speaks of [Lumastrazil](lumastrazil.md) as his older sister. Rumors concern her possession or servitude. Later notes and drafts describe relationships differently.

These facts describe source versions, not a complete selected Vaeloran biography. Vel’s adventure, specific reagents, the imprisonment’s cause and divine genealogy have not automatically transferred into game history.

## Open questions

Whether Colossumbra is fully dead, bound, intermittently conscious or described differently by different traditions remains unresolved for Vaelora. So do the origins of the petrification and the truth of his relationship to Lumastrazil. No claim makes him responsible for the [Low Spring](history.md) or the current water dispute.
