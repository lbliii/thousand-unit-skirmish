# Settlements of the Three Measures corridor

**Status:** working lore adapted from the foundation package. Exact map placement remains open.

These places share a proposed commercial relationship. Bellweather supplies grain overland; Ellionar and Sereward's water connection requires a plausible dryland margin. They are not established locations on Forked Vale.

## Pellit

A [Bellweather](regions/bellweather.md) market town around a weighing yard shaded by three oaks. Barley farms, pear orchards and sheep pasture supply grain, felt, tool handles and pear vinegar. Wells and a millpond serve the town. Imported metalwork and dependable transport keep its mills and deliveries working.

The yard assembly elects a seasonal weighkeeper. Tenant growers can speak, but owners can wait out bad prices. Miller Nella Imbray wants growers to pool deliveries. At the First Loaf supper, each oven contributes bread from the new crop; households also help bereaved neighbors complete essential field work.

Broker Deren Voskel advanced Fellowship reserves for flour destined for Tassel’s Ennd, concealing the size of the commitment. He needs mill parts on the returning convoy. The loaded flour is drawing damp under damaged covers; the larger reserve remains sound. Pellit’s stake in the [dispute](three-measures.md) is trust and continuity of milling as well as profit.

## Istrava

An inhabited [Ellionar](regions/ellionar.md) terrace settlement of garden beds, shaded courts, workshops and cisterns. Lapis tiles mark public channels; copper fittings are repaired repeatedly. Residents grow fruit, vegetables and an adapted grain, exporting glazed vessels, preserves and precise fittings.

A terrace council assigns maintenance turns. An elected gatewright can stop an unsafe gate. Apprentices begin with silt clearing and measurement before learning specialized horticulture, including proposed [hushlime](magic.md#hushlime). Lantern Pruning brings neighbors together on the steps while experienced growers tend fruit trees.

Gatewright Sava Mekren countersigned the repaired outlet before reviewing a later guarantee. Councillor Orel Tavin orders the next garden turn honored: newly grafted trees may not survive another missed watering. Both have reasons to delay concession; affected households distrust arguments that leave their plots dry.

## Tassel’s Ennd

A [Sereward](regions/sereward.md) caravan stop named for retired crews tying worn tassels to a beam. Residents repair harnesses, carts and containers, sell storage jars and provide lodging and guides. A fresh spring, catchment and deliveries supply drinking water. A separate caravan cistern receives supplementary channel water from Istrava. The saline pool outside town is not connected to drinking supplies.

Resident households choose a cistern keeper; crews appoint a road speaker. Visitors’ water decisions require both. Rumi Dassel reserves drinking allowances for people arriving on foot. Proprietor Tessa Orven supports that custom but demands reliable pack-water for commercial travel. Retired guides worry that traffic exceeds the stop’s capacity.

Local [Wayfarer hospitality](wayfarers.md) starts with water and a seat. The spring-name story survives in wet-season bells and cups left for travelers. Violet-fleshed tubers are a source-inspired menu proposal whose biology and original name remain open.

## Names

Working pronunciations: Pellit, PELL-it; Istrava, iss-TRAH-vah. Tassel’s Ennd remains under review because its spelling closely echoes a source-story naming pattern. These entries establish no invented language family.
