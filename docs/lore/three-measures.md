# The Three Measures dispute

**Status:** working local event; outcome and precise placement remain open.

The Three Measures agreement connects [Pellit, Istrava and Tassel’s Ennd](settlements.md) through grain reserves, passage guarantees and water obligations. Made after the [Low Spring](history.md), it supported useful cooperation for years.

## The incompatible promises

An older clause grants a fixed opening period for a caravan water branch. A later addition guarantees a minimum delivered quantity. Both garden and caravan branches draw from a limited spring reservoir. A repaired leak improves delivery, but crews extend opening to fill larger convoys under the quantity guarantee. The extra withdrawal leaves less for garden turns during a poor season.

Neither record is forged. Surveys measure different points: release at the reservoir and arrival at the caravan cistern. A joint test could establish losses, but not decide whose promise to break before the next turn.

## Parties and stakes

Istrava’s Orel Tavin closes the branch to protect newly grafted trees. Gatewright Sava Mekren wants a joint survey but signed the repair before checking the later guarantee. Tassel’s Ennd keeper Rumi Dassel refuses more convoys without pack-water; using the drinking reserve would endanger residents and travelers on foot.

Carrier Tessa Orven instructs her escorted crew to reopen at the older agreement’s hour while claiming the later quantity. Orel orders guards to prevent entry. The [Store Fellowship and Joined Road](institutions.md) disagree internally about priorities and force. Pellit’s Deren Voskel has concealed a reserve advance and depends on returning mill parts.

Violence threatens because named people issue incompatible commands. Neither an ancestry nor an entire settlement is collectively guilty. The current wiki leaves the outcome unwritten.

## Wider importance

The dispute illustrates an inheritance that worked and then failed under changed conditions. Better knowledge can help without making allocation painless. Its causes are local; no divine manipulation is established. A future scenario might use it, but this entry introduces no convoy, negotiation or civilian mechanics.
