## Editorial checks

All ten regions have a distinct entry, a source/status note, and links to relevant world topics. Cross-regional relationships describe trade or thematic connections unless geography has been established. Beliefs have identifiable holders. No page requires an unpublished creation myth to make sense of the present.

New working additions in this wiki include modest livelihoods for less-developed regions and organizing concerns for Continuance/Boughward. These extend the regional direction, not hidden findings in the source stories. They remain clearly marked.
