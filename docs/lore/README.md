# Vaelora lore wiki

Vaelora is a world of cultivated places, traveling peoples and tangible remnants of immense power. Its inhabitants maintain gardens, roads, households and traditions while disagreeing about what the past means and what they owe one another.

This is the working lore reference, begun 30 September 2026. The author's stories steer its voice and imagination; Vaelora can transform their histories. Begin with the [world overview](world.md).

## Reading status

**Selected direction** means a region, visual identity or world anchor already chosen in the project. **Working lore** means newly developed or adapted material, available to use consistently in drafts but still editable. **Belief** identifies an account held within the world. **Open** means the answer has deliberately not been supplied. A selected painting does not establish every implied inhabitant or object as history.

Each page puts status near the beginning and keeps its source note at the end. Page prose describes the setting; the [source register](sources.md) records editorial limits. New mechanics and precise playable geography are outside this wiki's claims.

## Maintaining entries

Give each important name one clear referent. Link an event to its consequences and a place to its inhabitants, needs and connections. Explain whose belief a disputed account represents. When a decision changes, update dependent entries and record the change in [sources](sources.md). A mystery may stay open; incompatible drafts should not masquerade as a mystery.

The [game bible](../game-bible.md) owns approved product intent, the [voice guide](../lore-voice-bible.md) guides prose, and the [art checkpoint](../art-direction/vaelora-v1/README.md) retains visual provenance. The earlier [foundation package](../lore-foundation-m1.md) and [story review](../lore-foundation-story-review.md) remain dated drafting records. The wiki is the current place to extend lore; writing additional scenes is optional.
