# Peoples and cultures

**Status:** principal cultural directions are selected for exploration; demographic histories and several adaptations remain open.

A people’s ancestry, learned practices, place of residence and political allegiance describe different parts of a life. None supplies a complete personality. The names below identify traditions and peoples under development, not automatically playable factions.

## Ellion’ar — sun elves

Ellion’ar is the working name for sun elves; [Ellionar](regions/ellionar.md) names the region associated with their garden-city direction. This spelling distinction is a working convention. The selected visual language includes stepped gardens, solar horticulture, lapis glazing and copper.

Working cultural development emphasizes cultivation as knowledge passed through apprenticeship, household experiment and civic maintenance. Ritual calendars and practical planting experience can agree without every grower holding the same religious explanation. [Istrava](settlements.md#istrava) develops one such community, not a model for every sun elf.

The source stories place sun-elven life in a temperate homeland. Vaelora’s dryland garden direction is an adaptation; it is not evidence that the manuscript homeland was secretly desert.

## Soman’ar — moon elves

Moon-elven traditions connect the [Sombral Mere](regions/sombral-mere.md) with forest lakes, nocturnal gardens, opals and [Lumastrazil](lumastrazil.md). Her silence gives rites and inheritance an unresolved character.

The source material describes depletion or extinction. Living communities depicted or suggested by concept art are not enough to settle survival in Vaelora. Descendants, custodians of another ancestry, remnants and an earlier time period remain different possible explanations. Do not assert one as settled while the others appear elsewhere as fact.

## Steorran’ar — star elves

Star-elven traditions are associated with the [Pale Meridian](regions/pale-meridian.md): observation, astronomical instruments and communal interiors in a strongly seasonal northern landscape. Long summer days belong to this setting as much as long winter nights.

Source notes describe lost star elves. The identity of present custodians is open. A working institution may preserve their observations without proving its members descend from the original makers. No shared origin event for all three elven peoples is selected.

## Humans

Humans participate in agricultural, trading and traveling cultures. The [Pellit](settlements.md#pellit) draft uses familiar household and market institutions; [Wayfarer traditions](wayfarers.md) provide another cultural direction. Neither is a universal human culture. Exact regional population proportions remain unwritten.

## Explored bull and jackal peoples

Bull/jackal peoples have been explored visually in association with Wayfarer craft. Their ancestry names, anatomy, origins and demographic position remain exploratory. The wiki does not infer temperament, occupation or religion from animal resemblance. Shared ceramics and textiles can indicate cultural exchange without implying shared biology.

## Relationships

A solar gardener can live beyond Ellionar; a human can help maintain an elven archive; a caravan household can have members with different ancestries. Such relationships are available directions, not a claim that every settlement already contains every people. [Institutions](institutions.md) should state membership instead of using ancestry as shorthand for politics.
