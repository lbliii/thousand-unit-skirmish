# Vaelora

**Status:** selected world name and broad creative direction; the connections below are working lore.

Vaelora contains places people have learned to inhabit well and places that still resist them. Orchards, shaded courts, winter rooms and caravan stops coexist with petrified forests, silent divine traditions and instruments whose makers are absent. The past survives in soil, craft, custom and argument.

[View the world atlas](README.md#world-atlas) and the regional concept images linked below.

## The inhabited world

[Bellweather](regions/bellweather.md) offers cultivated countryside; [Ellionar](regions/ellionar.md) gives cultivation a terraced, water-conscious form. [Sereward](regions/sereward.md) connects settlements through journeys and hospitality. The [Veyrholds](regions/veyrholds.md) and [Siltmouths](regions/siltmouths.md) suggest different demands on settlement: height and exposure in one, shifting water and access in the other.

The [Underbough](regions/underbough.md) is a woodland of roots and close paths. [Vesperra](regions/vesperra.md) carries a deeper canopy and a stronger supernatural character. The [Sombral Mere](regions/sombral-mere.md) ties forest lakes to lunar traditions, while the [Pale Meridian](regions/pale-meridian.md) preserves an astronomical inheritance on a cold plateau. [Ru’Lora](regions/ru-lora.md) is exceptional: a hazardous petrified interior around the remains of [Colossumbra](colossumbra.md).

These identities do not yet establish borders, rulers, distances or every river connection. Regional maps are visual references, not a surveyed political atlas.

## People and belonging

[Ancestry, culture and allegiance](peoples.md) overlap without being interchangeable. Sun-, moon- and star-elven traditions have distinct associations. Humans and the explored bull/jackal peoples participate in [Wayfarer craft](wayfarers.md). Movement, exchange and mixed households prevent any region from becoming a single uniform people.

[Factions](institutions.md) form around promises, constituencies and competing obligations. Neither an ancestry nor a cultural palette determines whom somebody must support.

## Power and uncertainty

[Magic](magic.md) meets people through materials, places, practices and relationships. Expertise is useful but unevenly distributed. Some techniques remain reliable while their explanations are disputed. No universal magical technology or complete pantheon has been established.

The fate of [Colossumbra](colossumbra.md), [Lumastrazil’s silence](lumastrazil.md) and the extent of lost elven peoples remain major historical questions. They do not secretly explain all politics. The [Three Measures dispute](three-measures.md), for example, arises from shortage, successful repair and incompatible promises.

## Present condition

There is no selected world-spanning empire or universal war. The initial wiki develops a trading relationship among [three settlements](settlements.md), alongside a broader regional reference. Its immediate conflicts concern access, stewardship and the uneven costs of cooperation. Other regions retain room for independent histories.
