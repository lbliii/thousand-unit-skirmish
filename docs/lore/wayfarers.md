# Wayfarer traditions

**Status:** selected shared craft direction; customs below are working lore.

Wayfarer names a cultural tradition of travel, storage, repair and hospitality associated with [Sereward](regions/sereward.md). It is not an ancestry, a single nation or the name of every person who journeys.

Indigo and coral textiles, durable ceramics, cisterns and caravan equipment give the tradition a recognizable material language. Well-kept containers are a form of security. Craft knowledge travels with people and is adapted at each stop.

## Hospitality and obligations

At [Tassel’s Ennd](settlements.md#tassels-ennd), a guest receives drinking water and a place to sit before questions. Commercial pack-water is allocated separately. The distinction allows generosity without pretending supplies are unlimited.

A crew may carry a repaired jar or mended strap to a household farther along the road. Children learn routes through places where somebody will recognize them. These are local working customs rather than universal rules binding every Wayfarer.

The [Joined Road](institutions.md#the-joined-road) formalizes some reciprocal obligations. People can share craft without joining it; an institution’s guarantees are not the whole culture.

## Materials and stories

Secondary notes for the author's Soul Bandit describe saline pools, violet-fleshed tubers, petrified-wood bowls and tasseled carts. They inspire this direction, but the original prose remains missing. The [spring-name story](magic.md#the-spring-name-story) provides one proposed local tradition, with several tellings rather than a universally accepted account.
