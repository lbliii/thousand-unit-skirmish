# Vaelora world building strategy and plan

Proposal, 30 September 2026. Build a rich world through inhabited places whose materials, customs and conflicts have consequences. Use the author's [voice](lore-voice-bible.md), the comparative [research](lore-research.md) and the [selected art](art-direction/vaelora-v1/README.md) together. This plan establishes a method and first writing outcomes; its suggested histories and conflicts remain proposals.

## Current deliverable: a connected lore wiki

The user's subsequent direction makes a basic, connected reference the current target. The [wiki](lore/README.md) now covers the world overview, all ten regions, principal peoples and cultures, institutions, historical outline, magic and divine anchors. The first writing tranche below records the earlier plan; its scene and scenario steps are optional future applications rather than requirements for wiki completion.

Extend the wiki through relationships: a region links to inhabitants and useful materials; an institution links to membership and disputes; an event links to consequences. Next work should reconcile a small set of open decisions and deepen the entries they affect. A complete creation myth, extensive story sequence or implemented campaign is unnecessary. Keep private source prose outside the repository and summarize influence with provenance.

## What the world should give players

A player should want to know who lives beyond a battlefield, understand why its routes and stores matter, and recognize traces of a larger history. Everyday work makes those stakes credible. Immense lost power provides depth, but people still decide how to live, whom to trust and what to fight over.

The RTS core remains the current product priority. Lore first supports map identity, objectives, craft, names and a complete faction's presentation. It does not require a campaign engine, unique economies for ten regions or a new magic simulation.

## Connect consequences at several scales

Write outward from a concrete situation. A useful sequence is **event → material consequence → livelihood → institution → disagreement → present action**. It is a writing aid, not a requirement that all stories share one cause.

An illustrative proposal: a repaired irrigation channel crosses land claimed under two incompatible records. The repair increases cultivation downstream, creates a maintenance burden upstream, and makes both communities' claims newly valuable. Guards arrive when mediation fails. The player can understand the crossing's importance before learning the old grant's history. A supernatural interpretation may deepen the dispute, but need not be its hidden true cause.

For a supernatural anchor, follow the same discipline. Colossumbra's remains have environmental effects and a sacred prohibition. Establish what those effects do and whom they affect before adding another deity. Maintain some unrelated histories, natural hazards and successful institutions. The world should contain causal networks, not one secret explanation for everything.

## Separate truth from belief and draft from decision

Use a small Markdown ledger for the first tranche; a custom lore database is unnecessary. Each entry should record:

- Stable referent and preferred spelling, plus common or specialist alternatives.
- Status: manuscript fact, selected game decision, adaptation proposal, in-world belief, or unresolved draft conflict.
- Source and version, with the specific scene or passage supporting it.
- Known relationships and chronology, including what remains unknown.
- Who believes it, how they learned it, and what observable evidence supports the belief.
- Changes and affected entries or assets.

The first ledger should distinguish the physical effects established in Colossumbra's manuscript from the rumors about Lumastrazil. The author-facing truth can remain intentionally unset. An open mystery is a legitimate status; contradictory drafts are a different status.

A later revision must say what changed, why and what depends on it. Additive detail is routine. A reveal should have earlier evidence and preserve meaningful choices. An adaptation should explicitly name its new continuity. A replacement of a published fact deserves a short change note and a dependency review. Preserve dated source records rather than editing history to make it appear consistent.

The user's direction on 30 September 2026 establishes the stories as deep creative inspiration, without requiring literal reuse. Develop Vaelora as its own world, steered by their voice, naming, humor, material imagination and thematic concerns. Specific characters, histories, extinctions and cosmological relationships become game facts only when deliberately selected. Manuscript differences remain useful source records rather than contradictions that Vaelora must inherit. The user also confirmed *The Soul Bandit* as a reference; its original prose still needs recovery.

## Give cultures more than a color and a god

For each developed culture, describe food and water, household life, work, craft, learning, governance, hospitality, death, festivals, humor and internal disagreements. Then connect it to at least one neighbor through something needed, traded or disputed. Describe what a successful ordinary year looks like.

Keep ancestry, cultural practice, settlement and political allegiance distinct. A sun elf can disagree with the solar priesthood; a human household can live in an elven city; a faction can have members in several regions. The current Common Hearth, Open Passage, Continuance and Boughward names remain working proposals. Test them against institutions and constituencies before assigning universal ideologies.

Myth research should answer concrete questions. Babylonian civic records are useful for land and authority; Inana's descent is useful for jurisdiction and the conditions of return. Neither dictates a species' anatomy or requires a costume collage. Give borrowed structures new circumstances, local materials and consequences.

## Regional opportunities

The second column records existing art direction. The third offers questions and new writing possibilities. None of those questions establishes a mechanic or historical answer.

| Region | Existing anchor | Next question or proposed use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bellweather | Oaks, pears, barley, sheep, cultivated countryside | What makes an ordinary harvest work? Which market, grazing custom or neighbor keeps farms connected? Let prosperity have its own character. |
| Underbough | Exposed roots, brambles, fungi, copper and burgundy | Who maintains paths and decides which trees can be cut? Separate customary woodland practice from actual supernatural obligation. |
| Sereward | Oasis plants, caravan animals, water storage and Wayfarer craft | Who keeps cisterns clean and guarantees passage? Use the Soul Bandit material as a source lead; do not confuse saline pools with drinking water. |
| Ellionar | Stepped gardens, irrigation, lapis glazing, copper | Who maintains terraces and allocates water? What happens when civic competence and priestly prestige disagree? Desert-city framing is an adaptation of the temperate story homeland. |
| Veyrholds | Wind-shaped pines, goats, raptors, iron and elevated settlements | How do passes, winter stores and imported goods connect the holds? Mountains need livelihoods as well as fortresses. |
| Pale Meridian | Cold plateau, astronomical instruments, warm interiors | Who tends the instruments if star elves are lost? Explore custodians, descendants or a different era before asserting living star-elven enclaves. |
| Siltmouths | Reeds, tidal roots, marsh tubers and estuary life | Which claims move with the water? How do fishers, growers and boat crews keep agreements useful? Avoid automatic swamp poverty or corruption. |
| Vesperra | Ancient canopy, fungi, rare supernatural inhabitants | Why do people enter, and what practical work occurs at its edge? Give its strangeness a different role from the Mere and Ru’Lora. |
| Sombral Mere | Lakes, lunar gardens, reflective fauna, Lumastrazil's silence | Who performs old rites, and what still works? Identify time period and inhabitants before treating painted settlements as surviving moon-elven communities. |
| Ru’Lora | Petrified jungle, toxic dust, salt caverns, immense remains, demon refusal | What can be safely observed from outside? Distinguish fringe proposal, interior prohibition and visual landmarks. Keep it exceptional. |

## First writing tranche

The [first foundation review package](lore-foundation-m1.md) now develops the creative principles, historical layers, three settlements, two institutions and three contrasting passages. It is the conversation's first writing milestone, L1; selection of canon and player-facing scenario proof remain subsequent outcomes.

Deliver each outcome independently, with its limits. These are proposed writing steps, not new gameplay milestones or implementation gates.

| Step | Deliverable | Completion check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Resolve sources | A compact source/canon ledger covering manuscript variants, selected adaptations, and missing Soul Bandit/Bloomers prose | Every foundational game claim has a source/status; inspiration is distinguished from adopted history. Missing sources remain visibly missing. |
| 2. Calibrate the voice | Revise this voice bible using a place vignette, an ordinary conversation, a myth and a short objective description | The user can identify what sounds right or wrong across different registers. The final samples do not all use one comic cadence. |
| 3. Establish historical boundaries | One page separating a few selected physical truths, relative historical order, public myths and intentionally unanswered questions | No living population contradicts a selected extinction event; no later villain secretly owns every past conflict. Exact dates are introduced only when useful. |
| 4. Develop an inhabited corridor | Three settlement briefs across Bellweather, Ellionar and the Sereward, with livelihoods, trade and a present dispute | Each place has something it produces, something it needs, ordinary pleasure, internal dissent and a link to a neighbor. Route/hydrology remain proposals until checked against the selected geography. |
| 5. Develop one political faction | One dossier for the first faction, testing an existing working name | Explain membership, recruitment, governance, public promise, material dependence and a dissenting constituency. Connect presentation to the current roster without changing simulation rules. |
| 6. Prove the world in a match | One authored scenario brief, then a bounded implementation using existing objectives | A fresh player can explain the immediate conflict and a meaningful objective. Optional lore adds depth; it is not needed to understand orders. Record build, map and player observation in QA when implemented. |
| 7. Expand through consequences | Develop the adjacent region or institution implicated by the pilot | New entries connect to existing needs/history and introduce at least one distinct ordinary practice. Avoid expansion through a list of unused gods. |

Recommended pilot: a proposed dispute over an irrigation crossing between an Ellionar garden settlement and a Sereward caravan community, connected to Bellweather by grain trade. This uses existing garden/oasis art opportunities and the author's interest in knowledge, obligation and practical inconvenience. It should be named after local place and people briefs exist. Do not declare that Forked Vale is this location or change its existing fiction without checking the scenario's current purpose.

A simpler Bellweather storehouse dispute is the fallback if terraces and desert props would obstruct a useful first slice. The acceptance target is an intelligible match with a specific place identity, not a complete culture's asset catalog.

## Limits on the first expansion

Keep the ten-region atlas as an art checkpoint. Retain selected names and useful paintings while making proposed demographic, historical and geographic assumptions explicit. There is no need to repaint maps merely because the text has unresolved lore.

Ru’Lora, the lost elven traditions and the missing goddess are strong long-term anchors. They can appear in rumor, imported material or a distant landmark while the first inhabited corridor develops. Avoid introducing a complete cosmic hierarchy, resurrecting peoples for roster convenience, or explaining away local politics as celestial puppetry.

The lore master maintains continuity, source attribution and writing calibration, and owns a useful lore outcome through integration. Follow the repository's ordinary author-owned workflow. Art, QA and other owners are not routine approval queues; contact an affected owner only when an actual shared contract or conflicting edit blocks work.

## Documentation ownership

The game bible owns approved product/world intent. This strategy owns the proposed writing sequence and method. The research file preserves dated evidence and source limits; the voice bible owns prose guidance. The art checkpoint retains selected maps, palettes and provenance. QA records later in-game observations. Add a short pointer to the roadmap when a writing outcome becomes an active production slice; do not replace the current RTS priorities with this research plan.

The next lore session should review the wiki's open decisions, select a few connected facts and deepen their affected entries. Recovering The Soul Bandit and Bloomers can refine the voice while that independent work continues. Additional calibration fiction is optional.
