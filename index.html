      FIELD GUIDE
      Controls
      
          
      Hold the arrow keys to move the camera. Middle drag or Space + drag also pans; scroll to zoom toward the pointer. Edge scroll is on by default and can be changed in Match Controls. Use the Center selection, Home base, and Fit map buttons on the battlefield.
      
          
      Touch controls: select friendly units, open Orders, choose Target battlefield, then tap a destination, enemy, or resource. Drag the tactical map to move the camera.
      
          
      Gather food or wood: select workers, then right-click a resource, or use Orders → Target battlefield and tap it. Build a Barracks or Archery Range from Build & train, then choose a clear site; the field hint explains why a spot is blocked.
