# Provenance

The shared humanoid core, Worker parts, and Barracks derive from the project-owned v0.1.2 Blender source `source/base_build_sample_v0_1_2.py`; the Worker toolhead is widened 1.25× within its existing batch. The Infantry shield and spear, steel-grey cap, Archer bow and quiver, and moss hood are original low-poly geometry authored by `source/build_frontier_character_pack_v2.py` in Blender 5.2.1 LTS. The deterministic RGBA8 atlas is project-authored by the same source. Barracks construction cloth and trim are recolored neutral. Azure's standard is a straight-cut pennant with one centered bar; Ember's is forked-tail with a split bar.

Terrain and preview-only tree/rock images are copied byte-for-byte from `assets/environment/frontier-v1/`; their existing internal license is retained. No paid provider, external model, or third-party game art was used.

All new content is licensed for internal review under `LicenseRef-Thousand-Unit-Skirmish-Internal-Review`. `SHA256SUMS.txt` records every pack file other than itself.
